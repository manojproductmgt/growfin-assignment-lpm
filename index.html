Prototype Walkthrough & Rationale

PROBLEM 1 ADDRESSED

Knowledge Evaporation (Screen 2)

When an AR analyst manually matches an unrecognised payer name (e.g. ASHFORD PMTS LTD to Ashford Holdings Inc), existing tools discard that decision. The payment lands in the queue again the following month.

The Meridian Solution: After manual clearing is completed, Meridian presents an inline card proposing to remember the payer based on 3rd repetition and verifiable bank evidence (same bank account ****4419, identical invoice billing, identical remittance syntax).

PROBLEM 2 ADDRESSED

Blind Trust & Partial Matching (Screen 3)

Legacy tools show only "Matched". Analysts don't trust black boxes, so they spend hours manually re-verifying matches.

The Meridian Solution:

  • Explainable Reasoning Block: Surfaces exact evidence the system had (saved payer, remittance OCR, exact lines).
  • Partial Invoice Clearing: Shows 2 clean invoices closed, with INV-4547 partly paid ($1,247.00 applied, $153.00 still open). Instead of holding the whole $12,880 in unapplied cash over a small difference, clean balances close automatically.
  • 1-Click Unapply Detection Path: A wrong match is only ever discovered when a person undoes it. The unapply action costs one click with no multi-step modal friction. Every extra step is a mistake the system never learns about.
